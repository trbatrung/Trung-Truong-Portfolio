# Tone & Voice

_Revised 2026-10-08 to match `positioning.md` (both audiences, leadership leads)._

## Voice in one line
First person, professional with personality. Not corporate, not quirky. Blunt, specific,
practical. Senior and selective, not eager.

## The central narrative
_Revised 2026-10-25._ Ethan starts every job with the problem: **what is this video actually
for?** The people, the tools and the cut follow from it. AI is one of those tools, not the pitch.
A reader should take away **problems solved and decisions made**, not a skill with any one kind of
tool. Ownership and the standard still run underneath.

## Do
- **State the decision, not the task.** Directed, owned, set, built, defined. Not produced,
  edited, cut, shot, graded.
- **Put the evidence next to the claim**, on the same screen.
- **Name concrete tools and systems.** Specificity is the credibility.
- **Short sentences.** Cut adjectives before you cut facts.
- **Plain English** over agency language.

## Don't
- **No em dashes** in copy. Minimal dashes in general. Restructure the sentence, or use a period.
  (Section labels like "01 — Selected Work" still use em dashes. Whether they go too is an open
  item for step 4.)
- **No marketing filler:** passionate, cutting-edge, seamless, innovative, storyteller at heart,
  elevate, unlock.
- **Don't inflate credit** (see the credit rules in `positioning.md`).
- **Don't make clients feel shut out.** Leadership leads, but a production or editing client should
  never feel the site is too grand for their job.
- **Don't sound available for anything.** Curated, not desperate.

## Style conventions
- Section labels: two-digit index plus name, mono, e.g. "01 — Selected Work".
- Uppercase mono micro-labels for eyebrows, tags and metadata.
- Outcome lines in amber, introduced by an arrow.
- Final deliverable is the **final master**, not the "final cut".
- Window title bars show a filename, lowercase mono, e.g. `dashboard.live`, `founder-intro.mov`.
  Filenames are the only lowercase copy on the site (heyclicky pass, 2026-10-25).
