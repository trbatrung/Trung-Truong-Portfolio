# Portfolio Copy Rewrite Brief

## What I want

Rewrite the **copy only** on my portfolio site (`Trung-Truong-Portfolio`). The site currently positions me as a video producer/editor. I'm applying for senior creative leadership roles that own creative direction across a function, so the copy needs to read at that altitude.

Do not redesign anything. Do not change the structure, the section order, the layout, or which projects are shown. Text only.

## The governing rule

**Every line should say what I decided, not what I did.**

Craft verbs (produced, edited, cut, shot, graded) describe someone you hire to execute. Decision verbs (directed, owned, set, built, defined) describe someone you hire to lead. My underlying work supports the second framing. The copy currently doesn't.

Test for each line: could the editor on that job have written this sentence about themselves? If yes, it's too low.

## Section by section

### Hero
Current: *"I produce and edit video. With AI where it helps, and real craft where it counts."*

Problem: "produce and edit" are execution verbs and they cap everything below. The AI half reads as a taste statement ("where it helps") when it should signal that I built the system that decides where AI helps.

Direction: ownership verbs, and a promise of a system rather than a preference. Give me 4 to 6 options at different levels of directness so I can pick. Keep it two sentences max.

### 01 Selected Work (project descriptions)
Current descriptions use editing language, e.g. finding the story and cutting the footage.

Direction: lead with the problem and the call I made, then the outcome. Same projects, same facts, higher altitude. Roughly: what the client needed, what I decided, what it produced.

**Do not inflate credit.** If a project was an edit, describe the creative decisions inside the edit. Do not call it a directed piece. Flag anything where you're unsure of my actual role and ask me rather than guessing.

### 02 Tools I've Vetted and 03 How I Think
Leave the substance alone. These are the strongest sections for the roles I'm targeting because most creative leads can't show a documented point of view on tooling.

Only change: consider moving them higher in the page order, and tighten any copy that undersells them as personal notes rather than a working philosophy.

### 04 AI-Augmented Production
Current: three capability demonstrations.

Problem: demos prove I can operate tools. Anyone can claim that now. What I need to prove is that I can set standards other people work to.

Direction: restructure into **one system case study** instead of three demos. Use the Edge8 code based animation pipeline. Structure it as:
- what the pipeline replaced and why that was a problem
- what it produces now and how
- what broke along the way and what I changed as a result

The failure part is not optional. It's the credibility anchor. Ask me for the specifics rather than inventing them.

### 05 About
Current bio leads with seven years and 100+ projects.

Direction: drop "100+ projects." Throughput is a freelance credential and it works against a leadership pitch. Replace with scope: founded and ran a 10+ person multimedia agency, and currently own a full production pipeline end to end. Keep the years.

## Tone rules

- No em-dashes. Minimal dashes generally. Restructure the sentence instead.
- Professional with personality. Not corporate, not quirky.
- No marketing filler: passionate, cutting-edge, seamless, innovative, storyteller at heart, elevate, unlock.
- Short sentences. Cut adjectives before you cut facts.
- Plain English over agency language.
